# Responsibility Continuity

## Purpose

Responsibility Continuity preserves accountability across autonomous decision-making.

Execution may be delegated.

Responsibility may not.

## Principle

Every autonomous action must be traceable to the human or organizational authority that accepted responsibility.

## Responsibility Chain

Organization
↓

Business Owner
↓

Decision Authority
↓

AI System
↓

Agent
↓

Sub-Agent

↓

Action

↓

Outcome

## Requirements

Every delegation records:

- Who delegated
- Who accepted
- Scope of authority
- Time
- Policy
- Evidence

## Verification

An independent reviewer must be able to reconstruct the complete responsibility chain from outcome back to the original authority.

## Failure Conditions

UNKNOWN_AUTHORITY

BROKEN_CHAIN

UNAUTHORIZED_DELEGATION

EXPIRED_AUTHORITY

OUT_OF_SCOPE

## Goal

Autonomous execution never breaks accountability.
